Getting Here

How to arrive

Fly in and rent a car. The drive to Skaneateles is gorgeous either way.

SYR

Syracuse Hancock International

~25 minutes from Skaneateles. The closest airport with direct routes from most cities.

ITH

Ithaca Tompkins Regional

~1 hour away. Smaller airport, beautiful drive north through Finger Lakes wine country.

🚗 Getting to the Venue

We recommend renting a car — you'll want the freedom to explore. Rideshare (Uber/Lyft) is available from SYR. Need an airport pickup? Fill out the travel form and we'll coordinate!

Questions

We've got answers

What's the dress code?
Outdoor cocktail / garden party. Something you'd feel great in on a lakeside lawn. Sundresses, linen suits, flowy fabrics — yes. The vibe is celebratory and joyful. Express yourself.
Food for dietary restrictions?
Yes — vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options will be available. Put any serious allergies in the notes section of the travel form.
What's the weather like in August?
Gorgeous. 70–85°F, low humidity, long golden evenings. Bring a light layer for after sunset. Quick afternoon thunderstorms are possible but usually brief.
Is there parking?
Yes, parking is available at The Woodlot. More logistics details will come closer to the date.
Can I bring kids?
This is an adult-focused celebration. If you have a specific situation, reach out directly and we'll talk it through.
Indoors or outdoors?
Ceremony is outside on the lakefront. The reception starts outdoors for the golden hour and moves inside as the evening cools. Comfortable shoes are your friend.
What should I bring for the weekend?
Swimsuit — the lake is crystal clear and you will want to be in it. Also: sunscreen, layers for the evening, comfortable shoes, and a camera.
