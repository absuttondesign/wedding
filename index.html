ITH

Smaller airport, beautiful drive north through Finger Lakes wine country. ~55 min to the furthest Airbnb lodging.

Questions

FAQs

What's the dress code?
Outdoor cocktail / garden party. The venue is three levels of decks with stairs — beautiful, but worth knowing for shoe choices. Sundresses, linen suits, flowy fabrics — yes. The vibe is celebratory and joyful. Express yourself.
Food for dietary restrictions?
Yes — we'll have options for a range of dietary needs.
What's the weather like in August?
Gorgeous. 70–85°F, low humidity, long golden evenings. Bring a light layer for after sunset. Quick afternoon thunderstorms are possible but usually brief.
Can I contribute for lodging?
Nothing is expected, anything helps. To get a rough idea of lodging costs, it's about $535 per bed, or $760 with boats per person for the weekend.
What's the nearest airport?
Syracuse Hancock (SYR) is closest — about 25 minutes away and has the most flight options. Ithaca Tompkins (ITH) is about an hour but a gorgeous drive through wine country. Either works!
How do I get from the airport to my Airbnb?
Fill out the travel form below — let us know your arrival details and whether you need a pickup. We'll coordinate from there.
How do I get to the Woodlot?
By boat! Everyone is staying at Airbnbs and cabins around the lake, and the final leg to the Woodlot is across the water. Boat rentals will be delivered directly to your accommodation. Airbnb details can be found in the Out-of-Towners Lodging section.
Indoors or outdoors?
Ceremony is outside on the lakefront. Reception on the deck. Comfortable shoes are your friend.
What about gifts?
In lieu of stuff, we have a honeymoon fund. Thanks!
What should I bring for the weekend?
Swimsuit — the lake is crystal clear and you will want to be in it. Also: sunscreen, layers for the evening, comfortable shoes, and a camera.
