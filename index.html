Getting Here

How to arrive

Fly in and rent a car. The drive to Skaneateles is gorgeous either way.

SYR

Syracuse Hancock International

The closest airport with direct routes from most cities. ~25 min to Skaneateles, ~40 min to the Glen Cove properties.

ITH

Ithaca Tompkins Regional

Smaller airport, beautiful drive north through Finger Lakes wine country. ~55 min to Skaneateles, ~45 min to Glen Cove.

Questions

We've got answers

What's the dress code?
Outdoor cocktail / garden party. The venue is three levels of decks with stairs — beautiful, but worth knowing for shoe choices. Sundresses, linen suits, flowy fabrics — yes. The vibe is celebratory and joyful. Express yourself.
Food for dietary restrictions?
Yes — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, and allergy-friendly options will be available. Garlic & allium allergies are specifically accommodated. Check all that apply in the travel form below so we can make sure you're taken care of.
What's the weather like in August?
Gorgeous. 70–85°F, low humidity, long golden evenings. Bring a light layer for after sunset. Quick afternoon thunderstorms are possible but usually brief.
What's the nearest airport?
Syracuse Hancock (SYR) is closest — about 25 minutes away and has the most flight options. Ithaca Tompkins (ITH) is about an hour but a gorgeous drive through wine country. Either works!
How do I get from the airport to my Airbnb?
Fill out the travel form below and let us know! Tell us if you need an airport pickup or if you're planning to rent your own car. We want to make sure everyone gets to the lake without stress.
How do I get to the Woodlot?
By boat! Everyone is staying at Airbnbs and cabins around the lake, and the final leg to the Woodlot is across the water. Boat rentals will be delivered directly to your accommodation. More details — including your specific Airbnb info — coming soon.
Can I bring my parents?
Yes! As long as they behave. The lake does not card anyone, but it does have standards. Little ones are especially encouraged to look for lucky stones in the pebbles along the shore.
Indoors or outdoors?
Ceremony is outside on the lakefront. The reception starts outdoors for the golden hour and moves inside as the evening cools. Comfortable shoes are your friend.
What should I bring for the weekend?
Swimsuit — the lake is crystal clear and you will want to be in it. Also: sunscreen, layers for the evening, comfortable shoes, and a camera.
