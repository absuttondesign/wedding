Getting Here

How to arrive

Skaneateles is a small town in the Finger Lakes. You'll want to fly into one of the nearby airports and rent a car or arrange a ride.

SYR

Syracuse Hancock International

About 25 minutes from Skaneateles. The closest major airport — most direct routes fly through here.

ITH

Ithaca Tompkins Regional

About 1 hour from Skaneateles. Smaller airport, fewer direct routes, but a beautiful drive through the Finger Lakes wine country.

🚗 Getting from the airport to the venue

We recommend renting a car — you'll want the flexibility to explore. Rideshare (Uber/Lyft) is available from SYR. If you need a pickup, let us know in the travel form below and we'll do our best to coordinate!

Questions

We've got answers

What's the dress code?

Outdoor cocktail / garden party. Think: something you'd feel great in while standing on a lakeside lawn. Sundresses, linen suits, flowy fabrics — yes. Stilettos on a grassy lawn — up to you (but maybe bring a backup). The vibe is celebratory and joyful. Express yourself.

Is there food for dietary restrictions?

Yes — we'll have vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options. If you have a serious allergy or specific need, please note it in your travel form and we'll make sure you're taken care of.

What's the weather like in August?

August in the Finger Lakes is beautiful — typically 70–85°F (21–29°C), low humidity, long golden evenings. Bring a light layer for the evening if you run cool. The occasional afternoon thunderstorm is possible but usually brief.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, there's parking available at The Woodlot. More details on logistics will come closer to the date. If you're staying onsite or nearby, you won't need to worry about a thing.

Can I bring kids?

This is an adult-focused celebration, but if you have a specific situation, reach out to us directly and we'll talk through it. We want everyone to be present and relaxed — including parents.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

The ceremony will be outside on the lakefront. The reception will be a mix — outdoors for the golden hour, and we'll move inside as the evening cools. Wear your comfy shoes.
